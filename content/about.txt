About ECG Means

ECG Means exists for one reader: the person holding an ECG report with a phrase on it they do not understand. Hospital and clinic ECG machines print automated interpretations that are technical, over-sensitive and rarely explained. We translate them.

## Who is behind it

All content is written and reviewed by Dr. Faisal Irshad, MBBS · Medical Doctor · PMDC 54440-S, a practising medical doctor with 18 years of clinical and laboratory-medicine experience. His registration can be checked with the Pakistan Medical and Dental Council (PMDC). ECG Means is an independent publication and is not affiliated with any hospital, device manufacturer or pharmaceutical company.

## How pages are produced

1. **Choosing topics.** Each page covers one phrase or finding exactly as it appears on real ECG printouts, chosen from the questions patients search for most.
2. **Research.** Definitions and thresholds follow standard references: the AHA/ACC/HRS recommendations for standardisation and interpretation of the ECG, the Fourth Universal Definition of Myocardial Infarction, Goldberger's Clinical Electrocardiography, NIH and StatPearls resources, and the Life in the Fast Lane ECG library. Each page lists the sources it relies on.
3. **Medical review.** Every page is reviewed by Dr. Irshad before publication for accuracy, clarity and safety. The review date appears at the top of each page.
4. **A clear verdict.** Every page states plainly whether the finding is usually harmless, depends on context, needs clinician review, or is a reason to seek prompt care, and when to get urgent help.
5. **Updates.** Pages are re-reviewed when guidance changes or a reader reports an error.

## Illustrations and calculators

The ECG strips on each page are schematic teaching drawings made for ECG Means, drawn on standard 25 mm/s, 10 mm/mV paper. They show the key feature of each finding and are not real patient tracings. The calculators use standard textbook formulas and run entirely in your browser.

## What we do not do

We do not interpret individual ECGs, offer diagnoses, or replace the clinician who ordered your test. Our aim is to help you understand the report and ask better questions.

## Corrections

If you spot an error or out-of-date information, email hello@ecgmeans.com with the page address. Confirmed corrections are made promptly and the page's review date is updated.
