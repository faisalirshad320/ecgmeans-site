About ECG Means
@@LEDE@@
ECG Means exists for one reader: the person holding an ECG report with a phrase on it they do not understand. Hospital and clinic ECG machines print automated interpretations that are technical, over-sensitive and rarely explained. We translate them.
@@BODY@@
## Who is behind it

All content is written and reviewed by Dr. Faisal Irshad, MBBS · Medical Doctor · PMDC 54440-S, a practising medical doctor with 18 years of clinical and laboratory-medicine experience. ECG Means is an independent publication and is not affiliated with any hospital, device manufacturer or pharmaceutical company.

## How pages are produced

- Each page covers one phrase or finding as it appears on real ECG reports.
- Definitions and thresholds follow standard references: the AHA/ACC/HRS recommendations for standardisation and interpretation of the ECG, Goldberger's Clinical Electrocardiography, and the Life in the Fast Lane ECG library.
- Every page states plainly whether the finding is usually harmless, context-dependent, in need of clinician review, or a reason to seek prompt care.
- Pages are dated and re-reviewed when guidance changes.

## What we do not do

We do not interpret individual ECGs, offer diagnoses, or replace the clinician who ordered your test. Our aim is to help you understand the report and ask better questions.
