@@BODY@@
## What "ST depression" means

The ST segment should sit level with the baseline. When part of the heart muscle is not getting enough blood, the ST segment in the leads facing that area drops below the line. During a stress test, ST depression that appears with exercise is the classic sign of a significant coronary narrowing.

ST depression is not always ischaemia. A thickened ventricle under strain, left bundle branch block, the "reverse tick" of digoxin, fast heart rates and low potassium all push the ST segment down. The shape helps: flat or down-sloping depression is more worrying than up-sloping.

## What it looks like on the ECG

The ST segment dips below the baseline, measured a short distance after the QRS. Horizontal or down-sloping depression of 0.5–1 mm or more in neighbouring leads is the pattern clinicians watch for.

## Common causes

- Reduced blood flow to the heart (angina, or a non-ST-elevation heart attack)
- Left ventricular hypertrophy with strain
- Left bundle branch block, paced rhythm
- Digoxin effect
- Fast heart rate (rate-related)
- Low potassium

## Should you worry?

Depends on context

New ST depression with chest pain, breathlessness or sweating can mean a heart attack and needs same-day assessment. Stable ST depression from a known thick ventricle or from digoxin is less alarming but still reported to a clinician. ST depression found on a stress test is an important result that guides further testing.

**Get urgent help** if this finding comes with chest pain or pressure, severe breathlessness, fainting, or a fast heartbeat that will not settle. Call your local emergency number if it comes with chest pain.

## What usually happens next

1. Comparison with previous ECGs and a troponin blood test if there has been chest pain
2. Review of digoxin and potassium
3. A stress test or angiography if ischaemia is suspected
4. Treatment of the underlying cause

### Questions to ask your doctor

- Is this new compared with my previous ECG?
- Could it be my blood pressure medicine, digoxin or potassium?
- Do I need a stress test or a troponin check?
@@FAQ@@
Q: Does ST depression mean a heart attack?
A: It can be a sign of reduced blood flow, including a non-ST-elevation heart attack, but it has several other causes. The context and a troponin test decide.
Q: Is ST depression on a stress test serious?
A: It is the classic sign of a significant coronary narrowing and usually leads to further testing, though the degree and leads matter.
Q: Can ST depression be benign?
A: Yes. Digoxin, a fast heart rate, a thick ventricle and lead factors can all cause it without an acute problem.
