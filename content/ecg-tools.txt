## About these tools

Each calculator uses the standard formulas found in ECG textbooks and runs entirely in your browser, so nothing you type is sent anywhere. They assume standard ECG paper (25 mm/s and 10 mm/mV), which is what almost every machine prints.

The tools are for understanding and learning, not for diagnosis. An automated machine reading, a hand measurement and a calculator can each give slightly different numbers. A value near the edge of a normal range is common in healthy people, so it is always judged together with symptoms, medicines and the rest of the tracing by the clinician who ordered the test.
