Medical Disclaimer
@@LEDE@@
The information on ECG Means is general educational content about electrocardiogram findings. It is not medical advice, does not create a doctor–patient relationship, and cannot take account of your history, symptoms, medications or the actual appearance of your tracing.
@@BODY@@
ECG findings must always be interpreted by a qualified clinician in the context of the whole person. Never disregard professional advice or delay seeking it because of something you have read here.

If you have chest pain or pressure, severe or sudden breathlessness, fainting, or a very fast or very slow heartbeat with dizziness, call your local emergency number immediately.

We make every effort to keep content accurate and current, but medical knowledge changes and errors are possible. Use of this site is at your own risk.
